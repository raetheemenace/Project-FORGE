# Product Overview

FORGE is a cloud-native multimodal lab management platform designed for the Technological Institute of the Philippines - Manila. It serves as a transactional ledger system for tracking high-value laboratory equipment and inventories across multidisciplinary facilities.

## Core Purpose

Solve laboratory equipment management challenges in touch-inefficient environments where users wear gloves or have occupied hands. The system provides real-time asset tracking with multimodal interaction (voice, vision, haptic feedback) for hands-free operation.

## Key Features

- Real-time equipment tracking and reservation system
- AI-powered equipment identification using image recognition (AWS Bedrock Claude 3)
- Multimodal HCI: voice commands, camera scanning, haptic feedback
- ACID-compliant transaction ledger preventing concurrency conflicts
- Progressive Web App (PWA) for cross-platform accessibility
- Audit trail with high-resolution equipment images stored in S3

## Target Users

- Students and faculty managing lab equipment reservations
- Lab administrators tracking inventory and usage
- Academic staff monitoring equipment availability

## Business Context

Academic project for Information Management, HCI 2, and Platform Technologies courses. Demonstrates enterprise-grade cloud architecture, database design, and modern web development practices.
